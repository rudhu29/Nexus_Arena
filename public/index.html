TACTICAL COMBAT GUIDE

1. THE HUNT (1vAll Asymmetric Mode)
• Boss: 650+ HP, devastating melee shockwave attack, and Fortress Shield or Shadow Blink!
• Hunters: Equip Assault, Sniper, or Vanguard loadouts. Use Sonar Radar to reveal the Boss and coordinate your squad!
2. SKIRMISH (Symmetric Core Clashes)
• Symmetrical Blue vs Red. First to 12 points wins!
• Energy Cores: Golden orbs spawn on the map. Collecting one awards +1 Team Point and grants an Overcharge boost! Eliminating rivals gives +2 points.
3. SIEGE & MOMENTUM SURGE
• Hold Zones Alpha, Bravo, Omega to generate points. First to 100 points wins!
• Momentum Surge: Trailing teams receive an electric Surge aura (+25% move speed & +25% fire rate) keeping comebacks within reach!
4. SEARCH & DESTROY (COD / BGMI CQB)
• Attackers (Red): Grab the EMP bomb case, push Site A or B, and hold to plant (~2.2s). Guard until detonation (40s countdown)!
• Defenders (Blue): Prevent the plant or eliminate all attackers. If planted, rush the site and defuse (~2.5s) to win!
• Tactical Rules: Single life per round (no respawn until round reset). First team to 3 round wins takes the match!
5. GUN GAME (FFA WEAPON MASTER)
• Progression: Tier 1 (Dual Blasters) → Tier 2 (Scatter Shotgun) → Tier 3 (Railgun) → Tier 4 (Plasma Cannon) → Tier 5 (Golden Blade).
• Objective: Score an elimination to immediately unlock the next weapon tier. First operative to score a melee kill with Tier 5 wins!
6. CYBER OUTBREAK (ZOMBIE INFECTION)
• Infected Alpha: High speed (3.8), toxic leap pounce, stealth cloaking, and venom melee claws.
• Survivors: Must survive 2:30 min. Every fallen survivor transforms into an infected! If one survivor remains, they trigger Last Survivor Standing (+100 Shield, Overcharge).
7. CORE HEIST (CAPTURE THE FLAG 3v3)
• Rules: Infiltrate enemy vault, grab their Quantum Core, and sprint back to your base.
• Scoring: You can only score if your own friendly core is safe at base! Touch dropped friendly cores to immediately return them. First team to 3 captures wins!
ARENA MAPS & TELEPORTERS
• Neon Metropolis: Classic urban pillars with tight cover angles.
• Cyber Core: High-tech reactor bunkers with open cross-lanes.
• Omega Ruins: Ancient cyber chambers with Quantum Teleporters that warp players across the arena!
• Sector 7 Warehouse: COD & BGMI CQB shipping containers, crate barricades, and Sites A & B!
